## 4. Slide Layout Rules

Every slide uses one of three zone layouts:

### Layout A — Cover / Engagement / CTA (no card)
```
[Top: handle — small, light]
[Middle: large display headline, centred]
[Middle-lower: single thematic icon or illustration]
[Bottom: handle or CTA element]
```

### Layout B — Content slide (with card)
```
[Top: slide headline in display font, centred]
[Gap]
[Main: rounded white/light card, full-width with padding]
  [Inside card: sub-heading (bold) + bullet points]
[Bottom: handle]
```

### Layout C — Media slide (photo + card)
```
[Top: header label e.g. "NEW LISTING" or project name]
[Middle: property photo in rounded frame]
[Bottom: rounded card with key specs, centred text]
[Footer: handle]
```

**Global layout rules:**
- Card corner radius: 16–20px (rounded, soft)
- Card padding: 20–24px internal
- Max 4 bullet points per card — if more content, split into two slides
- Consistent top/bottom margins across all slides in a post
- All slides in one post must use identical background, font, and spacing

### POST TYPE 1 — Educational Carousel
**Accent colour:** Brown `#3D2B1F`
**Slide count:** 7–8 slides
**Target audience:** First-time buyers, HDB upgraders, property-curious
**Goal:** Saves, follows, DM enquiries

| Slide | Layout | Headline | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A — Cover | Emotional hook or surprising number | Single thematic icon. NO body text. Hook must create fear, curiosity, or surprise — not label the topic. |
| 2 | B — Content | "What does it mean?" | Definition in 2–3 bullets. Plain language. A first-timer must understand immediately. |
| 3 | B — Content | "Why does this exist?" | Context behind the rule/concept. 3–4 bullets. Adds depth without being dry. |
| 4 | B — Content | "Who does this apply to?" | Segmented list: SC / PR / Foreigner / Entity. Use bold sub-headings inside card. |
| 5 | B — Content | "How much?" or "The breakdown" | Actual figures, rates, timelines. Most-saved slide — make data scannable. Use sub-headings. |
| 6 | B — Content | "What this means for you" | Practical takeaway. Bridges education to action. Example: "If you're an SC buying your 2nd property, you'll need to factor in 20% ABSD on top of your purchase price." |
| 7 | A — Engagement | "What are your thoughts?" | Mascot character illustration. Sub-line: "Let us know in the comments." |
| 8 | A — CTA | Specific soft offer | Example: "Not sure how ABSD affects your next purchase? DM me your situation." + WhatsApp 90401882 + CEA No. R011448D |

**Cover slide formula:**
> Emotional trigger OR surprising number → short promise sub-line → handle
> Example: "Buying a 2nd property? You could owe $200,000 in taxes." (NOT: "ABSD")

## 6. Cover Slide Formula

**The rule:** Never label the topic. Always lead with tension, curiosity, or a number that surprises.

| Post Type | Weak (old) | Strong (new) |
|---|---|---|
| Educational | "ABSD" | "Buying a 2nd property? You could owe $200,000 in taxes." |
| Educational | "CONDO PURCHASE TIMELINE" | "Most first-time buyers get this wrong. Here's the actual order." |
| New Launch | "CANBERRA CRESCENT RESIDENCES" | "OCR condo starting from $1.2M. Here's everything you need to know." |
| Market Insight | "Q1 2025 PROPERTY UPDATE" | "Prices just went up 3.9%. Good news or bad news for you?" |
| Myth-bust | "CPF FOR CONDO" | "You can use CPF to buy a condo. But nobody tells you the catch." |

**Cover slide anatomy:**
```
LINE 1: Hook (8–12 words max) — number, fear, or bold claim
LINE 2: Sub-line (optional) — short promise: "Here's what you need to know."
[Icon or illustration — one, centred, thematic]
FOOTER: @SGLAUNCHHOMES
```

## 7. Caption Formula

### Instagram / TikTok
```
[LINE 1] Hook — 1 sentence. Surprising stat, fear, or bold claim. No period — let it hang.

[LINE 3–5] 2–3 lines of the core value from the post. Plain language. Speak to one person.

[LINE 7] Save this before your next property appointment.
[LINE 8] Follow @sglaunchhomes for weekly Singapore property breakdowns.

[HASHTAGS] #sgproperty #singaporecondo #sglaunchhomes #sgrealestate #singaporeproperty #condosingapore #sgpropertymarket #firsttimehomebuyer #hdbupgrade #sgcondo
```

### RedNote (Xiaohongshu)
```
[TITLE] Keyword-rich, search-optimised Chinese title
Examples: 新加坡买公寓必知的5个费用 / 外国人在新加坡买房攻略 / 新加坡ABSD详解

[BODY] Personal, informative, conversational Chinese.
Write as if explaining to a friend. Use line breaks generously.
Keep to 150–250 characters for best readability.
Bilingual is acceptable — Chinese first, English key terms in brackets.

[TAGS] #新加坡买房 #新加坡公寓 #新加坡房产 #sglaunchhomes #新加坡投资 #新加坡楼市
```

## 11. Brand Voice Reference

| Situation | Say this | Not this |
|---|---|---|
| Explaining a rule | "Here's how it works in plain terms." | "In today's complex property landscape..." |
| Sharing data | "Prices went up 3.9% last quarter." | "The market is experiencing unprecedented growth." |
| CTA | "DM me your situation — no pressure." | "Call now to book your exclusive private viewing!" |
| Engagement prompt | "Which one applies to you — drop it below." | "What are your thoughts? Let us know!" |
| Caption hook | "Most buyers don't know this until it's too late." | "In this post, we'll be covering ABSD." |

**Tone rules:**
- Write for one person, not a crowd
- Explain like a knowledgeable friend, not a brochure
- Never hype, never pressure, never corporate
- Short sentences. Active voice. Real numbers.
