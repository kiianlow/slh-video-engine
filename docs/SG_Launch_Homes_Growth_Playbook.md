## 0. How we measure success
Not followers. Not views. These:
- Conversations started (DMs / WhatsApp)
- Lead magnet downloads / opt-ins
- Appointments booked
- Viewings arranged
- Clients acquired
- Commission earned

> A 300-follower account that books 3 viewings a month beats a 10,000-follower account that books none. We optimise for the bottom of that list, not the top.

## 1. The core problem
You have content but no funnel. Views currently lead nowhere. Before scaling content, we install a capture-and-convert system. Content without a funnel is a hobby; content with a funnel is a business.

**Three fixes, in order of leverage:**
1. Install the funnel (capture point + nurture).
2. Pick one lead platform; repurpose to the others.
3. Build trust as far as faceless allows, then sequence the face.

- Most competitors stay generic. Owning a clear niche makes you the obvious choice for that specific person.

**Highest-value play:** RedNote investors/foreign buyers. Biggest cheques, but only after you localise (see §6).

## 4. The lead funnel: Viewer → Follower → Engaged → Lead → Client

```
VIEWER      hook stops the scroll
   ↓        (CTA: implicit — keep watching)
FOLLOWER    a reason to come back: a named series + consistency
   ↓        (CTA: "Follow for [series]")
ENGAGED     comment / save / Story poll
   ↓        (CTA: "Comment WORD" / "Save this")
LEAD        captured: comment-keyword → DM → WhatsApp/opt-in
   ↓        (CTA: "Comment COST and I'll send it")
CLIENT      nurtured 1:1 → free consult → viewing → close
            (CTA: "Want this for your situation? WhatsApp me")
```

**The capture mechanism you're missing:** a keyword-to-DM bridge.
- Post ends with: *"Comment [KEYWORD] and I'll send you [magnet]."*
- They comment → you DM the magnet → conversation starts in DM → move to WhatsApp 9040 1882.
- Every comment = a warm lead + an engagement signal that boosts reach. It does two jobs at once.

## 6. Lead magnets — ranked by buyer-seriousness

The point of a magnet isn't downloads. It's *qualification*: a casual scroller won't download a financing calculator — only someone about to transact will. Pick magnets that filter for intent.

| Magnet | Attracts | Seriousness filter | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **New launch price list / project guide** | Investors, upgraders | **Highest** — only near-buyers want pricing | Low (you have access) |
| **Upgrader cashflow planner** ("can you afford the jump?") | HDB upgraders | High | Medium |
| **ABSD / financing calculator** | Investors, 2nd-property buyers | High | Medium (build) |
| First Condo Checklist (PDF) | First-timers | Medium | Low (Canva) |
| Quarterly market report | Broad | Low (top-funnel) | Medium |

**Most likely to attract serious buyers:** new launch price guides + affordability/ABSD calculators. They self-select for purchase intent. Lead with these.

**Start now (zero/low build):** "Ask me anything — no pitch" WhatsApp CTA + a Canva *Upgrader Checklist*. Build the calculator in Phase 2.

## 9. Content-to-lead framework (apply to every post)

For each piece, define four objectives + the CTA:

| Objective | Question | Example CTA |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Will a stranger stop? | (hook does this — no CTA) |
| Engagement | Will they react? | "Which one are you — drop it below" |
| Trust | Did they learn something real? | "Save this before your next viewing" |
| Conversion | Is there a next step? | "Comment COST for the free calculator" |

Rule: one primary CTA per post. Awareness/trust posts → soft CTA (save/follow). Conversion posts → keyword-to-DM.

## 10. Trust-building as a younger, (for now) faceless agent

**Authority without selling:**
- Teach, cite official sources (URA, HDB, CPF Board, MAS) — borrowed authority.
- Use real numbers, not adjectives. "Prices rose 3.9%," not "the market is booming."
- Be the one who explains the scary stuff calmly. Clarity = perceived competence.

**Social proof (start collecting immediately):**
- Screenshots of real questions you've answered (anonymised).
- "Helped a client work out their ABSD this week — here's the breakdown."
- Testimonials the moment you close anything, even small.

**Younger-agent angle — turn it into an edge:**
- Position as: transparent, digital-native, no old-school pressure tactics.
- Caution: faceless undercuts the young-agent advantage (they can't see you're sharp and likeable). **Voiceover is your minimum trust signal** — your voice carries competence faceless can't. Use it on every video.

**The faceless ceiling (honest):** reach and saves don't need a face. *Conversion* eventually does. Plan to introduce voice now → face later.

## 11. Roadmap: creator → authority → brand

**Phase 1 — Foundation (Months 0–3) · Goal: first inbound leads**
- Fix all 3 profiles (bio, SEO, RedNote phone number, Highlights).
- Install funnel: link-in-bio + WhatsApp + one lead magnet + keyword-to-DM.
- Make IG the lead platform; go faceless video with voiceover.
- Localise RedNote fully (Chinese covers + titles).
- *Measure: DMs started, magnet downloads.*

**Phase 2 — Authority (Months 3–9) · Goal: predictable lead flow + first content-attributed clients**
- Lock one signature series.
- Launch Telegram channel ("SG New Launch Alerts") for nurture.
- Build the affordability/ABSD calculator.
- Start collecting + posting social proof and testimonials.
- Introduce your face (avatar → talking head).
- *Measure: viewings booked, leads per month trending up.*

**Phase 3 — Brand (Months 9–24) · Goal: brand-driven inbound, higher-value clients**
- Be the recognised "SG upgrade / property-explained-straight" name in your niche.
- Referrals + repeat investor clients (esp. RedNote).
- Consider a lightweight content engine (batch + repurpose) or help.
- Premium positioning: known niche authority commands better clients.
- *Measure: % of clients arriving via brand/referral, average commission value.*
