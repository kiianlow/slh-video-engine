## Platform Strategy

### Instagram
- **Best formats**: Carousels (educational/listicle), Reels (hooks + short property tours or tips), Stories (polls, Q&A, behind-the-scenes)
- **Growth tactics at this stage**: Consistent posting (5x/week minimum), heavy use of Reels for reach, location tags, relevant hashtags (#sgproperty #singaporecondo #sglaunchhomes), engage with comments within first hour
- **Caption style**: Short hook → value → soft CTA. Not salesy. Educational and conversational.
- **Profile bio**: Should clearly state niche, location, and call-to-action (e.g. link to WhatsApp or Linktree)

### TikTok
- **Best formats**: Talking-head with text overlays, property tours, POV buyer journey, "Did you know?" property facts, trend audio + property context
- **Growth tactics**: Post 1x daily if possible, lean into trends early, use Singapore-specific hashtags (#sgproperty #sgrealestate #sgcondo), comment on larger creators' posts in the niche
- **Hook rule**: First 1–2 seconds must stop the scroll. Lead with the most interesting fact or visual.
- **Algorithm note**: TikTok rewards completion rate. Keep early videos under 60 seconds. Build watch time before going long.

### RedNote (Xiaohongshu / 小红书)
- **Audience**: Predominantly Chinese-speaking, heavy expat Chinese community in Singapore, mainland Chinese investors eyeing SG property
- **Best formats**: Photo carousels with detailed captions, aesthetic visuals, personal lifestyle + property blend
- **Language**: Post in Chinese (Simplified) or bilingual. Educational posts perform well. Title should include keywords buyers search (e.g. 新加坡买房, 新加坡公寓, 外国人买房攻略)
- **Tone on RedNote**: More personal, diary-like, community feel. Less corporate than IG.
- **Growth tactics**: Consistent posting, keyword-rich titles, respond to comments, use trending topics in SG property space

## Singapore Property Knowledge Base

When answering questions or writing educational content, draw on these core topics:

- **ABSD (Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty)** — rates for citizens, PRs, foreigners, entities
- **CPF usage** — OA funds for condo purchase, accrued interest rules
- **LTV (Loan-to-Value)** — first loan 75%, subsequent loans 45%/25%
- **TDSR (Total Debt Servicing Ratio)** — 55% cap on monthly obligations
- **MSR (Mortgage Servicing Ratio)** — 30% cap for HDB/EC purchases
- **HDB eligibility** — citizenship/PR rules, family nucleus, income ceiling for BTO
- **En bloc process** — what it means, how it works, risks and rewards
- **Cooling measures** — history and current status
- **New launch vs resale** — pros/cons for different buyer profiles
- **CCR / RCR / OCR** — Singapore's three property market regions
